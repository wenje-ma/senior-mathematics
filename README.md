# Senior Mathematics (Senior-High, PEP 2019)

> 95 weeks of lesson notes and fully worked exercises covering the complete PEP A (2019) senior-high mathematics curriculum — five textbooks from compulsory volumes 1–2 through elective volumes 1–3 — with R-generated figures and a complete English translation.

Content follows the **人教 A 版 (2019)** senior-high series. Textbook scans in `textbooks/` are named `10.1`/`10.2` (compulsory vols. 1–2) and `11.1`/`11.2`/`11.3` (elective vols. 1–3); `textbooks/page_mapping.md` records printed-page ↔ PDF-page offsets and per-section page ranges, since the material was transcribed page by page against the scans.

## The notes

The reference is split into two files:

- **notes1.md** — sets and logic, quadratic functions/inequalities, functions and their properties, exponential & logarithmic functions, trigonometric functions, vectors, complex numbers, solid geometry, statistics, probability;
- **notes2.md** — space vectors & solid geometry, lines and circles, conic sections, sequences, derivatives, counting principles, random variables and their distributions, statistical analysis of paired data.

In total 2,345 definitions and 391 theorems, bold-tagged with `$…$`/`$$…$$` mathematics. As in the other repositories, the notes hold only definitions/theorems; all worked examples live in `homework/`.

## The homework

Each `homework/weekN.md` follows the fixed format:

1. **Toolbox** — a blockquote listing the chapter's definitions/basic facts/conclusions;
2. **Exercises** — textbook exercises and review sets;
3. **Solutions** — complete worked solutions ending with `$\blacksquare$`.

95 weeks total, with some weeks devoted to whole-chapter review sets. Representative topics: sets, periodic functions, vectors, space geometry, random experiments, space vectors, ellipses, sequences, derivatives, counting principles, continuous random variables, linear regression.

## The figures

340 figures are generated by R scripts in `codes/` (paired `cairo_pdf` + `svglite` output, Chinese labels in SimSun, English code comments), stored in `figures/` and referenced from the Markdown. Scripts are run manually; there is no build automation.

## Bilingual layout

- `cn/` — authoritative Chinese version.
- `en/` — complete English translation of both notes files and all 95 weekly homework files, line-count-identical with the Chinese originals.
